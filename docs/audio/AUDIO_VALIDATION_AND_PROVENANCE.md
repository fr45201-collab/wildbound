# Audio Validation and Provenance Specification

## 1. Provenance & Intellectual Property Standards

All audio assets in Wildbound adhere to strict provenance rules:
1. **100% Original Synthesized Assets:** Every sound effect, voice, footstep, and musical composition is generated by custom procedural synthesis code in `tools/audio_generation/`.
2. **Zero Third-Party Downloads:** No sounds are extracted from commercial games, sound libraries, YouTube, or stock audio platforms.
3. **No Trademark or Melody Infringements:** Musical scales and chord progressions avoid recognizable copyrighted melodies or signature leitmotifs.
4. **License:** All assets are registered under `CC0 1.0 Universal` (Public Domain Dedication) in `assets/manifests/asset_manifest.json`, granting unconditional commercial use.

---

## 2. Automated Quality Assurance & Validation

Audio quality and file integrity are enforced through `tools/audio_generation/validation/validate_audio.py`.

### Automated Checks:
1. **File Integrity:** Reads RIFF/WAV chunks via Python standard `wave` module. Unreadable or corrupt headers fail validation immediately.
2. **Sample Rate Verification:** Every asset must match $44,100\text{ Hz}$ exactly.
3. **Bit Depth Verification:** Must be signed 16-bit linear PCM (`sampwidth == 2`).
4. **Channel Allocation:**
   - Short SFX, Footsteps, Combat, Creatures $\to$ 1 Channel (Mono for spatial processing).
   - Ambience Loops, Music Tracks $\to$ 2 Channels (Stereo for immersive spatial breadth).
5. **Silence Detection:** Unpacks PCM amplitudes to verify $\text{Peak} > 0$ and $\text{RMS} \ge 1.0$. Dead or empty files fail immediately.
6. **Clipping Prevention:** Normalization pass scales amplitudes to peak $\le 0.95$ ($\approx -0.45\text{ dBFS}$) to eliminate digital inter-sample clipping.
7. **Loop Boundary Energy Check:** `validate_loops.py` tests that initial and terminal sample blocks match within $6.0\text{ dB}$ RMS to ensure click-free looping.
