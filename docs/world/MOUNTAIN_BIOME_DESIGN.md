# Wildbound — Mountain Biome Design (Iron Crag Highlands)

## 1. Geographical Identity & Aesthetic Direction

* **Biome Name**: Iron Crag Highlands (`mountain`)
* **Color Palette**: Heather moorland turf (`#8c8a56`), granite ledge gray (`#5a636e`), volcanic basalt dark gray (`#2b2f36`), and rushing cascade cyan (`#64b5f6`).
* **Visual Theme**: Rugged alpine peaks, scree slopes, mountain ridges, sheer granite cliffs, cascading mountain streams, and fortified stone watchtowers overlooking treacherous mountain passes.

## 2. Tileset Specifications

* **Asset Key**: `mountain_tileset`
* **File Path**: `assets/tilesets/mountain/mountain_tileset.png`
* **Resolution**: 256×256 px (8×8 grid of 32×32 px tiles, 64 total tiles).
* **Key Tile Classes**:
  * *Highland Heather Turf*: Wind-scoured grass and alpine moss cling to thin soil.
  * *Granite Scree & Crags*: Broken rock fragments, sharp ledges, and sheer cliff walls.
  * *Mountain Rill & Cascade*: Fast-flowing cold mountain spring water and foaming shallows.
  * *Cut-Stone Fortress Masonry*: Heavy interlocking ashlar blocks for high-altitude bastions.
  * *Highland Pines*: Stunted windblown evergreen trees resilient to harsh winds.

## 3. Prototype Map Specifications

* **Map File**: `maps/mountain/mountain_01.json`
* **Tile Dimensions**: 60×45 tiles (1920×1440 world pixels).
* **Key Landmarks & Geographic Zones**:
  1. *Western Iron Crag Ridge*: Towering sheer granite cliffs forming a natural barrier.
  2. *Highland Pass Road*: Cobbled mountain road winding between ravines.
  3. *Northern Highland Watchtower*: Strong stone bastion guarding the mountain pass.
  4. *Cascade Gorge*: Rushing torrent cutting through rocky channels into an alpine basin.
* **Objects & Spawners**: 22 map objects including watchtowers, pine trees, stone boulders, and mountain birds.

## 4. Atmospheric Configuration

* **Weather**: Highland Wind / Montane Gale (`alpine_wind`)
* **Fog Density**: 0.20 (crisp high-altitude air with light drifting cloud wisps).
* **Cloud Layer**: Rapidly moving mountain cloud sheets blowing at 24.0 units/sec.
* **Ambient Lighting Tint**: Clear, piercing alpine daylight (`#f0f5ffa0`).
