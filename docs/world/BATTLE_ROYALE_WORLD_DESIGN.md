# Wildbound — Battle Royale World Design

## 1. Vision & Core Philosophy

The "Isle of Wildbound" is designed as a cohesive, lore-rich fantasy Battle Royale environment. Rather than a set of disjointed biome rectangles stitched together arbitrarily, the world is conceived as a geographically natural volcanic and continental archipelago island.

The island provides:
1. **Diverse Tactical Environments**: Open long-range desert sands, dense ambush-heavy forest groves, restricted marshland duckboard corridors, rugged vertical mountain passes, and open snowfields.
2. **Organic Geographical Transitions**: Rivers flowing from mountain snowmelt and highland waterfalls into lowland forest rivers, which widen into estuaries and coastal ocean bays. Sand dunes transitioning into dry savannah and rocky foothills.
3. **Strategic POI Distribution**: 5 major settlements, 14 strategic points of interest, 45 dedicated loot locations, and 12 perimeter/inland spawn zones ensuring balanced player drops, varied looting paths, and active mid-game rotations.
4. **Natural Chokepoints & Escape Routes**: River bridges, chasm crossings, and narrow boardwalks create high-tension combat decision points while providing alternative natural flanking routes.

## 3. World Design Principles Implemented

### 3.1 Geographical Believability
- **Hydrological Flow**: Precipitation and glacial melt in the northern Iron Crag peaks form cascading waterfalls that feed into the Great Forest River, flowing southwest into the ocean. The desert remains arid due to the northern mountain rain shadow effect.
- **Topographical Gradient**: Low-lying coastal swamps in the northwest ascend into alpine highlands, which gradually descend eastward into sub-zero taiga basins and south into arid sandstone plains.

### 3.2 Combat Space Dynamics
- **Long-Range Engagement Zones**: Sunscorched Wastes and Frostfall Taiga offer open lines of sight ($> 600\text{ px}$) favoring ranged bow play and scouting.
- **Close-Quarter Combat (CQC)**: Greenwood Village interior streets, Mirewood hut clusters, and Ancient Fortress interior ruins offer tight corners ($< 150\text{ px}$) favoring melee weapons and surprise ambushes.
- **Concealment & Stealth**: Forest foliage canopy, swamp cattail patches, and rock clusters allow players to break line-of-sight and shake off pursuing AI or hostile opponents.

### 3.3 Battle Royale Match Flow Preparation
- **Early Game (Deployment & Looting)**: 12 distributed spawn regions around the perimeter allow squads to drop safely or contest high-tier POIs (e.g. Greenwood Town Hall or Ancient Citadel).
- **Mid Game (Rotations)**: Players rotate along primary highways or cut across nature trails toward the central Ancient Fortress.
- **End Game (Final Circles)**: Any biome on the island can support an intense final circle showdown with balanced cover (trees, rocks, ruins, elevation props).
