# Wildbound — Asset Acquisition Catalogue (Gate 1 Baseline)

## 1. Overview & Strategy

This catalogue categorizes all visual asset needs for Wildbound across the 14 core game areas identified in Phase 49. It acts as the master candidate register for phased acquisition.

---

## 2. Master Acquisition Register by Category

### Category 1: Player Characters & Modular Appearance
- **Target Specs**: Humanoid 32×48 px frame, 4-directional (Down, Left, Right, Up), 1:1 pixel ratio.
- **Identified Candidates**:
  - Superpowers Character Pack (CC0)
  - OpenGameArt LPC Modular Base Frames (CC-BY 3.0)
- **Status**: `DISCOVERED`

### Category 2: Modular Clothing & Armor
- **Target Specs**: Layered spritesheets matching 32×48 px humanoid walk/idle cycles.
- **Identified Candidates**:
  - LPC Armor and Cloaks collection (CC-BY 3.0 / CC0 portions)
  - Kenney Roguelike Equipment overlays (CC0)
- **Status**: `DISCOVERED`

### Category 3: Hairstyles & Accessories
- **Target Specs**: Modular hair layers with facing-dependent depth sorting.
- **Identified Candidates**:
  - LPC Hairstyle Pack (CC-BY 3.0 / CC0)
- **Status**: `DISCOVERED`

### Category 4: Enemies & Creatures
- **Target Specs**: 4-directional top-down walk, attack, hurt, death animations. Sizing tiers: Small (16×16), Standard (32×32), Large (48×48 / 64×64).
- **Identified Candidates**:
  - Superpowers Monsters (Slimes, Skeletons, Goblins) (CC0)
  - CraftPix Free 2D Monster Pack (CraftPix Free License)
  - OpenGameArt Fantasy Bestiary (CC0 / CC-BY)
- **Status**: `DISCOVERED` (Slime selected for Pilot)

### Category 5: Wildlife & Ambient Fauna
- **Target Specs**: 2-directional or 4-directional wandering ambient sprites (Deer, Rabbits, Frogs, Fish).
- **Identified Candidates**:
  - OpenGameArt Forest Animals (CC0)
- **Status**: `DISCOVERED`

### Category 6: Biome Tilesets (Desert, Snow, Swamp, Mountain, Forest)
- **Target Specs**: 32×32 px seamless terrain autotiles with elevation cliff faces.
- **Identified Candidates**:
  - Kenney Roguelike / RPG Desert & Dungeon Tiles (CC0) (Selected for Pilot)
  - OpenGameArt 32×32 Roguelike Biome Tilesets (CC0)
- **Status**: `DISCOVERED`

### Category 7: Buildings & Architectural Structures
- **Target Specs**: 3/4 oblique top-down buildings with exterior footprints and cutaway interior roofs.
- **Identified Candidates**:
  - Superpowers Medieval Town structures (CC0)
  - Kenney Tiny Town modular buildings (CC0)
- **Status**: `DISCOVERED`

### Category 8: Weapons & Combat Props
- **Target Specs**: 32×32 icons, ground drop items, and held weapon sprites.
- **Identified Candidates**:
  - 7Soul 16×16 & 32×32 RPG Weapon Sprites (CC0 / CC-BY 3.0)
  - Game-icons.net Weapon Vectors & Bitmaps (CC-BY 3.0)
- **Status**: `DISCOVERED`

### Category 9: Items & Resources
- **Target Specs**: 32×32 item catalog icons (Potions, Scrolls, Runes, Gems, Materials).
- **Identified Candidates**:

### Category 10: Environmental Animations
- **Target Specs**: Looping animated tiles (Water ripples, waterfall cascades, campfire flames, chimney smoke).
- **Identified Candidates**:
  - Superpowers Animated Elements (CC0)
- **Status**: `DISCOVERED`

### Category 11: Particle Effects
- **Target Specs**: 8×8 to 16×16 alpha particle billows (sparks, dust puffs, magical sparkles, blood hits).

### Category 12: UI Elements & HUD Chrome
