# Wildbound — Weapon Asset Catalog (Phase 15 Production Collection)

## 1. Overview

This catalog documents the complete initial collection of **11 distinct weapon designs** (6 swords, 5 bows) and **1 supporting projectile asset** authored for Wildbound. All assets have been registered in the Asset Management System (AMS) manifest and validated.

---

## 2. Swords Collection (6 Designs)

### SWORD-001 — Wooden Training Sword (`sword_wooden_training_001`)
* **Category / Subcategory**: `sword` / `training`
* **Visual Description**: A blunt training sword fashioned from seasoned birch wood with natural grain texturing, a rounded tip, simple wooden crossguard, and rough cord-wrapped hilt.
* **Intended Purpose**: Novice sparring, starter gear, zero lethal hazard appearance.
* **Palette**: Ash wood `#a67c52`, golden grain `#c49a70`, shadow `#785030`, cord `#c2b280`.

### SWORD-002 — Rusty Short Sword (`sword_rusty_short_001`)
* **Category / Subcategory**: `sword` / `shortsword`
* **Visual Description**: A short, double-edged iron blade recovered from damp earth. Features chipped cutting edges, dark iron oxidation, and prominent red-orange rust flecks.
* **Intended Purpose**: Scavenger weapon, worn early-game find, bandit sidearm.
* **Palette**: Tarnished iron `#757d75`, rust orange `#9c4e23`, dark oxidation `#4b524b`.

### SWORD-003 — Iron Sword (`sword_iron_001`)
* **Category / Subcategory**: `sword` / `arming_sword`
* **Visual Description**: Standard fantasy arming sword with a crisp, balanced double-edged iron blade, broad straight crossguard, brown leather-wrapped grip, and round disk pommel.
* **Intended Purpose**: Standard soldier weapon, benchmark fantasy sword aesthetic.
* **Palette**: Forged iron `#a8b1b8`, blade highlight `#cfd7dc`, dark guard `#464e56`, leather hilt `#543217`.

### SWORD-004 — Steel Longsword (`sword_steel_longsword_001`)
* **Category / Subcategory**: `sword` / `longsword`
* **Visual Description**: A refined, elongated double-handed longsword forged from bright folded steel. Features a polished fuller, slender cruciform guard, dark wire grip, and gilded brass pommel.
* **Intended Purpose**: Knightly armament, high-tier martial weapon.
* **Palette**: Polished steel `#e0e6ed`, edge shine `#ffffff`, dark wire `#2b333d`, gold pommel `#d4af37`.

### SWORD-005 — Curved Sword (`sword_curved_001`)
* **Category / Subcategory**: `sword` / `saber`
* **Visual Description**: An elegant, swept scimitar-style single-edged blade designed for swift slashing attacks. Adorned with a sculpted brass quillon and polished ebony handle.
* **Intended Purpose**: Desert nomad armament, duelist saber, alternative curved silhouette.
* **Palette**: Swept steel `#bccad6`, bright highlight `#e6f0f8`, brass quillon `#c5a059`, ebony grip `#222222`.

### SWORD-006 — Ancient Rune Sword (`sword_ancient_rune_001`)
* **Category / Subcategory**: `sword` / `relic`
* **Visual Description**: A mythic sword forged of dark star-metal, etched with glowing cyan celestial runes along the blade fuller. Emits an ethereal arcane aura and features a sculpted dragon-wing guard.

## 3. Bows Collection (5 Designs)

### BOW-001 — Wooden Hunting Bow (`bow_wooden_hunting_001`)
* **Category / Subcategory**: `bow` / `hunting_bow`
* **Visual Description**: A light, flexible self-bow carved from straight-grained ash wood with tapered tips, braided hemp bowstring, and minimalist leather arrow-rest wrapping.
* **Intended Purpose**: Traditional starter ranged weapon, hunter aesthetic.
* **Palette**: Natural ash `#9c7a4c`, light grain `#bf9968`, hemp string `#e6dec8`.

### BOW-002 — Longbow (`bow_longbow_001`)
* **Category / Subcategory**: `bow` / `longbow`
* **Visual Description**: A towering yew longbow designed for heavy draw weights. Features distinctive heartwood-sapwood lamination, polished horn tip nocks, and a fine linen string.
* **Intended Purpose**: High-range archer weapon, disciplined military archer design.
* **Palette**: Aged yew `#6e371b`, sapwood `#8b4a26`, horn nocks `#dcd0b3`, linen string `#f4f1ea`.

### BOW-003 — Reinforced Bow (`bow_reinforced_001`)
* **Category / Subcategory**: `bow` / `composite_bow`
* **Visual Description**: A sturdy, heavy-duty composite bow reinforced with tempered iron bracing clasps, thick leather risers, and steel-core wire stringing.
* **Intended Purpose**: Durable, armored ranger armament; heavy kinetic punch.
* **Palette**: Iron plates `#5a636e`, brace highlights `#828e9b`, dark timber `#5f3c23`, grip `#3b2210`.

### BOW-004 — Recurve Bow (`bow_recurve_001`)
* **Category / Subcategory**: `bow` / `recurve_bow`
* **Visual Description**: A compact equestrian recurve bow with forward-curving limb ends, bone reinforcement horn tips, and a textured leather grip.
* **Intended Purpose**: Agile skirmisher weapon, high rate-of-fire visual character.
* **Palette**: Cherry composite wood `#a0522d`, horn tips `#e8e4c9`, silk string `#ffffff`.

### BOW-005 — Ancient Rune Bow (`bow_ancient_rune_001`)
* **Category / Subcategory**: `bow` / `runic_bow`
* **Visual Description**: An ethereal elven bow sculpted from enchanted ironwood. Pulsating emerald-green life runes glow along its upper and lower limbs, linked by a humming beam of pure magical force string.
* **Intended Purpose**: Relic-tier ranged weapon, ancient elven artifact.
* **Palette**: Ironwood `#2c3e50`, arcane runes `#38ef7d`, life aura `#11998e`, force string `#a8ff78`.
